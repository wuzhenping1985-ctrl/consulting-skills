# Financial Metric Playbook

## Profitability

- Revenue growth = revenue / prior revenue - 1. Split into price, volume, mix, new customers, churn, and FX where possible.
- Gross margin = gross profit / revenue. Diagnose by product mix, procurement cost, yield, labor, utilization, discounts, freight, and inventory write-downs.
- EBITDA margin = EBITDA / revenue. Use for operating performance, but reconcile major adjustments.
- EBIT margin = EBIT / revenue. Better when depreciation reflects meaningful asset intensity.
- Net margin = net income / revenue. Diagnose operating profit, interest, tax, minority interests, and one-offs.

## Efficiency

- Inventory days = average inventory / COGS * 365. Diagnose forecast accuracy, slow-moving stock, production planning, and purchasing policy.
- Receivable days = average AR / revenue * 365. Diagnose customer mix, credit policy, billing, disputes, and collections.
- Payable days = average AP / COGS * 365. Diagnose supplier terms, bargaining power, and payment discipline.
- Cash conversion cycle = inventory days + receivable days - payable days.
- Asset turnover = revenue / average assets. Diagnose capacity utilization and asset-light versus asset-heavy model.

## Returns And Capital

- ROIC = NOPAT / invested capital. Diagnose margin, capital intensity, tax, working capital, and asset utilization.
- ROE = net income / average equity. Decompose with DuPont: net margin * asset turnover * equity multiplier.
- Net debt / EBITDA and interest coverage indicate leverage capacity, but adjust for leases and one-offs when material.

## Cash Flow

- Operating cash flow conversion = operating cash flow / EBITDA or net income. Diagnose working capital, capex, provisions, deferred revenue, and non-cash items.
- Free cash flow = operating cash flow - maintenance capex. Separate growth capex when relevant.
- Capex intensity = capex / revenue or capex / depreciation. Diagnose growth phase, replacement cycle, and asset quality.

## Diagnostic Questions

- Is the problem revenue, margin, cost structure, capital efficiency, cash collection, or financing?
- Is the change structural or temporary?
- Which segment, product, customer, geography, or channel explains the movement?
- Is management controlling the driver, or is it external?
- What action changes the metric, and how quickly?
