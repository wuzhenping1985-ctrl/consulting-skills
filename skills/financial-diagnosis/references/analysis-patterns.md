# Financial Analysis Patterns

## Revenue Bridge

Use when explaining revenue movement:

1. Start with prior-period revenue.
2. Add volume change from existing customers or products.
3. Add price/rate change.
4. Add mix change.
5. Add new business.
6. Subtract churn/lost business.
7. Add FX, scope, and accounting effects separately.

## Margin Bridge

Use when margin changed materially:

- Price realization
- Product/customer/channel mix
- Input cost inflation or deflation
- Yield, scrap, utilization, and labor productivity
- Logistics, warranty, and service cost
- Inventory write-downs or one-offs

## Peer Benchmarking

Benchmark only after checking comparability:

- Business model and revenue recognition
- Geography and currency
- Product mix and customer concentration
- Scale and lifecycle stage
- Lease, depreciation, and capitalization policy
- Listed-company versus private-company disclosure differences
