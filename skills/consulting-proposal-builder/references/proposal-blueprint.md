# Proposal Blueprint

Use this reference for full consulting proposal drafts. Adapt headings to the client's industry and project context.

## Input Checklist

Collect or infer:

- Client basics: name, ownership, region, business lines, revenue/asset/headcount scale, lifecycle stage, competitive position.
- Stated needs: decision-maker concerns, trigger event, target deadline, expected deliverable, internal sponsor, affected departments.
- Current situation: financial performance, profitability, growth pressure, organization chart, workforce mix, incentive practices, process issues, digital systems, governance/control model.
- Time-series data: for listed companies or clients with available data, collect at least three years of comparable revenue, profit, margin, cash flow, asset/liability, headcount, productivity, business mix, regional mix, customer/channel, and operating metrics where relevant.
- External context: industry cycle, policy/regulation, customer and channel changes, technology shifts, competitor moves.
- Constraints: budget, timing, data availability, internal sensitivity, stakeholder alignment.

## Full Structure

### 1. 需求理解

#### 1.1 行业发展现状判断

Suggested logic:

- `长期周期`: look beyond the latest year; use multi-year history to judge cycle position, growth quality, volatility, and structural shifts.
- `宽产品边界`: include upstream/downstream categories, adjacent products, substitutes, solution bundles, and customer use cases when they affect the client's opportunity or pressure.
- `广市场范围`: compare local, national, regional, and global markets where relevant; identify whether the client's market is leading, lagging, differentiated, or converging.
- `量化趋势`: use at least three years of comparable quantitative indicators to prove the trend where available, such as market size, CAGR, year-on-year growth, penetration rate, concentration ratio, average price, sales volume, capacity utilization, investment amount, financing amount, export/import value, customer count, or profitability.
- `宏观/政策`: regulatory tone, public funding, industrial policy, compliance pressure.
- `市场/客户`: demand growth, segmentation, customer buying criteria, price pressure.
- `竞争/标杆`: concentration, leading-company moves, new entrants, substitutes.
- `技术/运营`: digitalization, automation, AI, supply chain, product/service changes.
- `管理启示`: what capabilities companies must build now.

Write as judgment, not encyclopedia. Avoid judging the industry only from the most recent year or a narrow product definition unless the project scope explicitly requires it. Each major trend claim should be supported by at least one quantitative indicator and at least three years of comparable data when available. Do not use a single-year data point to conclude that the industry is rising, declining, concentrating, upgrading, fragmenting, or shifting. If only one or two years of data are available, state the limitation and use cautious language such as `初步判断` or `仍需进一步数据验证`. End with 2-4 management implications.

#### 1.2 同行企业解决思路

For each peer:

- Company and relevance: leading, similar scale, similar ownership, similar problem, or similar development stage.
- Public practice: what the company changed.
- Management logic: why the practice addresses the problem.
- Transferable lesson: what the client can borrow, with limits.

Case standard:

- Use named companies. Write `XX公司在...背景下，通过...机制/动作，解决了...问题` instead of generic claims.
- Include specific actions, such as organization restructuring, business unit setup, budget mechanism, product portfolio adjustment, channel reform, incentive redesign, digital system rollout, process reengineering, cost program, or governance cadence.
- Distinguish public fact from consultant inference. If the result is not publicly verifiable, present it as a likely management implication, not as proven performance.
- Do not count unnamed industry observations as peer cases.

Prefer a compact table when there are multiple peers:

| 企业 | 面临问题/背景 | 具体做法 | 管理逻辑 | 对本项目启示 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

#### 1.3 客户现状简要剖析

Structure by the user's facts. Common lenses:

- Development stage: startup/growth/scale expansion/maturity/turnaround/transformation.
- Finance: revenue growth, margin, cash flow, budget control, cost structure, investment efficiency.
- Human resources: talent density, key roles, skill gaps, incentives, succession, workforce efficiency.
- Organization: headquarters-subsidiary control, department interfaces, decision rights, spans/layers, governance cadence.
- Operations/process: core process breakpoints, duplication, cycle time, quality risk, cross-functional collaboration.
- Digital/data: system fragmentation, data quality, analytics capability, automation potential.

Preferred diagnosis method:

- For financial and operating diagnosis, apply `customer-diagnosis-framework.md`. Start from `经营成效` to judge whether scale growth and profit/return quality are real and matched; then use `经营安全` to judge whether debt, cash flow, and growth structure are sustainable.
- Combine financial and business perspectives. Financial indicators should lead to business questions about market strategy, product strategy, customer/channel strategy, operating efficiency, organization control, and resource allocation.

Data-period rule:

- If the client is listed, use public filings, annual reports, investor presentations, stock exchange disclosures, and comparable databases to build at least a three-year view wherever feasible.
- If the client provides internal datasets, use at least three years of comparable data when available; align accounting periods, organization scope, product definitions, and metric caliber before drawing conclusions.
- Analyze trend, structure, volatility, inflection points, and peer gaps. Do not rely on a single year to diagnose performance unless data availability is limited.
- When only one or two years of data are available, state the limitation and frame conclusions as hypotheses to validate through interviews or additional data requests.

Deep-diagnosis rule:

- Do not simply list revenue, profit, margin, assets, liabilities, or headcount figures that can be read directly from reports.
- Decompose reported changes into drivers. Examples: revenue growth from price, volume, product mix, customer mix, region mix, channel mix, new stores/projects, M&A, or accounting caliber; profit pressure from gross margin, raw material cost, labor cost, selling expense, R&D, G&A, finance cost, impairment, depreciation, or scale effect; cash pressure from receivables, inventory, payables, capex, prepayments, or working-capital cycle.
- Explain the contradiction behind numbers. Examples: revenue grew but profit did not; sales volume rose while unit price fell; gross margin improved but cash flow worsened; headcount expanded faster than revenue; new business grew but dragged down profitability.
- Convert financial or operating symptoms into management pain points, such as pricing power decline, weak product portfolio, channel efficiency loss, cost-control mechanism failure, budget rigidity, organization accountability gaps, poor project profitability control, data latency, or incentive misalignment.
- When data is insufficient for root-cause confirmation, state 2-3 competing hypotheses and list the additional data or interviews needed to verify them.

Use `现象 - 影响 - 初步成因 - 待确认事实` for sensitive or uncertain diagnoses.

#### 1.4 管理模块问题及目标

Convert diagnosis into 3-6 modules. For each module:

- Problem statement: concrete and management-oriented.
- Root cause hypothesis: why the issue exists.
- Project objective: target state and measurable direction.
- Key outputs: deliverables the consulting project will produce.

Example format:

| 管理模块 | 当前问题初判 | 解决目标 | 主要成果 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 组织管控与权责机制 | 总部与业务单元权责边界不清，决策链条长 | 建立分层分类授权与经营例会机制 | 权责清单、授权矩阵、会议与报表机制 |

### 2. 技术方案

Repeat the following for every module.

#### 2.x.1 整体方法论

Include:

- Framework name and one-sentence purpose.
- 3-5 dimensions.
- Diagnostic questions.
- Analytical tools.
- Expected outputs.

Keep the framework practical enough to become a work plan.

#### 2.x.2 外部同行做法

Map peer evidence to methodology dimensions:

| 方法论维度 | 同行企业做法 | 可借鉴点 | 对客户适配判断 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Do not include a case unless it supports the module's method.

#### 2.x.3 针对客户的解题设计

Write dimension by dimension:

- Current client issue or hypothesis.
- Analysis work to perform.
- Design approach.
- Key workshops/interviews/data needed.
- Deliverables.
- Expected management effect.

When facts are missing, provide 2-3 alternative solution hypotheses and state what evidence would choose among them.

## Output Styles

### Outline

Use numbered headings and bullets. Include assumptions and data needs.

### Proposal-Ready Prose

Use short paragraphs and consulting language. Tables are acceptable for module summaries and benchmark cases.

### PPT Skeleton

Use one page per logical claim:

- Page title as conclusion.
- 2-4 bullets as evidence or implications.
- Suggested chart/table for each page.

### Word Proposal

Use formal section headings, concise paragraphs, and tables for peer comparisons, module mapping, workplan, and deliverables.

Default final Word output should be multiple DOCX files:

| File | Content boundary |
| --- | --- |
| `01_需求理解_<客户简称>.docx` | Industry judgment, peer benchmarking, client diagnosis, management module split, and objectives |
| `02_技术方案_<模块名称>_<客户简称>.docx` | Technical方案 for module 1 only |
| `03_技术方案_<模块名称>_<客户简称>.docx` | Technical方案 for module 2 only |
| `...` | Continue according to the actual number of management modules |

Each technical方案 DOCX should include:

- Module problem and target recap from the demand-understanding document.
- Overall methodology for this module.
- External peer practices mapped to the methodology.
- Client-specific solution design by methodology dimension.
- Consulting work steps, required materials/interviews, deliverables, and expected management effects.

Do not create a combined `技术方案` Word file unless the user explicitly asks for combined delivery.

## Final Quality Gate

Before delivering, check:

- The industry section ends with management implications.
- The industry section uses a broad enough view: longer time horizon, wider product/category boundary, and broader market comparison where relevant.
- Industry trend claims are supported by quantitative data where data is available, and trend judgments use at least three years of comparable data rather than a single-year data point.
- Peer cases are relevant to the client problem, name specific companies, and explain what each company actually did.
- Listed-client or data-rich client diagnosis uses at least three years of comparable data where feasible and does not overstate single-year evidence.
- Client diagnosis goes beyond directly listed report figures and decomposes business drivers, structural contradictions, and real management pain points.
- Client diagnosis uses facts, cautious inference, and `待确认` markers.
- Every management module has a problem, objective, and deliverable.
- Every technical module includes methodology, peer practices, and tailored solution design.
- Solutions are specific enough to become project work steps.
- Final Word files are split into one `需求理解` document plus one `技术方案` document per management module.
