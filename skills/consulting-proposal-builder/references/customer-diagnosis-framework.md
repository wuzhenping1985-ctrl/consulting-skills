# Customer Diagnosis Framework

Use this reference for the `客户现状简要剖析` section. It is based on the user's two articles on `盘家底`: `经营成效` and `经营安全`.

## Core Logic

Analyze the client from two financial-operating lenses:

1. `经营成效`: whether the company has achieved real, high-quality scale growth and benefit growth.
2. `经营安全`: whether the company can sustain operations under debt, cash flow, and growth-structure pressure.

Do not treat finance as isolated accounting analysis. Convert financial findings into business and management questions: market strategy, product strategy, customer/channel structure, internal operating efficiency, organization control, governance cadence, and resource allocation.

## 1. 经营成效

经营成效 focuses on `规模变化` and `效益变化`. Use it to test whether growth is meaningful, profitable, cash-generating, and aligned with the core business.

### 1.1 Scale Indicators

Use selectively. The goal is not to list every number, but to identify whether scale growth is real and valuable.

| Indicator | How to analyze |
| --- | --- |
| 营业收入 | Test growth quality. Decompose into main business vs. non-main business, price vs. volume, product/customer/channel/region mix, one-off trading or pass-through revenue, and whether revenue growth matches profit and cash flow. |
| 营收增速 | Treat abnormal high growth as a signal to investigate business substance, accounting recognition, consolidation changes, trading business, engineering pass-through, or financing needs. |
| 主业营收占比 | Use for diversified groups to judge whether growth comes from strategic core business or low-quality peripheral activity. |
| 销售额/订单额 | Use when revenue recognition lags actual business activity, such as presale, project-based, or long-cycle industries. |
| 资产规模 | Distinguish table assets from off-balance-sheet managed assets or business operating scale. Test whether asset expansion comes from profit retention, shareholder injection, debt expansion, M&A, revaluation, or managed-area/customer expansion. |

Key rule: scale growth is not automatically good. If revenue grows without profit, cash, or core-business strengthening, treat it as a quality warning.

### 1.2 Benefit Indicators

Use benefit indicators to judge whether the company is truly creating economic value.

| Indicator | How to analyze |
| --- | --- |
| 利润总额/净利润/归母净利润 | Test profit quality. Check whether profit comes from recurring operations, non-recurring gains, fair-value gains, asset disposal, capitalization of expenses, impairment reversal, or consolidation effects. |
| 净利率/毛利率 | Decompose margin changes by product mix, pricing, raw material cost, labor cost, manufacturing cost, sales expense, R&D, G&A, finance cost, depreciation, impairment, and scale effect. |
| ROE/归母ROE | Use to judge shareholder return. Adjust or caveat for non-recurring gains, equity inflation, minority interest, capital injections, and high-quality business profits shared with minority investors. |
| 周转率 | Use total asset turnover, inventory turnover, and receivables turnover where relevant. Link turnover to asset efficiency, inventory pressure, receivable collection, supply-chain position, and business model. |
| 收现比/利现比 | Use operating cash inflow/revenue and operating cash flow/net profit to test revenue and profit quality. High revenue or profit without matching cash flow is a warning. |

### 1.3 Relationship Analysis

Do not analyze indicators independently. Link them to expose contradictions:

- Revenue grows rapidly but profit does not: test low-margin trading, pass-through business, price cuts, cost increases, weak expense control, or accounting recognition.
- Profit grows but operating cash flow does not: test receivables, inventory, non-cash fair-value gains, asset disposal, capitalization, or delayed collection.
- Asset scale grows but ROE falls: test debt-driven expansion, inefficient assets, revaluation, idle assets, or weak operating returns.
- Net profit is acceptable but归母净利润 is weak: test minority interests, social capital participation, or high-profit businesses outside the parent's effective return.
- Turnover improves but margin falls: test whether scale was exchanged for price, credit policy, or low-quality volume.

Recommended output format:

| Finding | Evidence | Driver analysis | Management implication | Data/interview to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 2. 经营安全

经营安全 focuses on operational sustainability. It is often more important than short-term operating performance for highly leveraged, transformation-stage, investment-heavy, infrastructure, real estate, construction, and local state-owned enterprises.

Analyze from three dimensions: `债务水平`, `现金流水平`, and `增长结构`.

### 2.1 Debt Indicators

| Indicator | How to analyze |
| --- | --- |
| 资产负债率 | Do not accept the surface number directly. Check debt nature, asset quality, revaluation, cross-shareholding, consolidation scope, minority interests, deferred capitalization, and whether liabilities are interest-bearing or operating liabilities. |
| 净负债率 | Calculate `(有息负债 - 货币资金) / 净资产` where data permits. Use it to judge pressure from interest-bearing debt after available cash. |
| 长短债比 | Test maturity structure and refinancing pressure. Excess short-term debt implies near-term repayment risk even if total leverage looks acceptable. |
| 融资成本 | Compare trend with LPR, credit environment, rating changes, and peer financing cost. Financing cost that falls slower than market rates may indicate credit pressure. |
| EBITDA利息倍数 | Use cautiously when profit quality is weak. It is useful only after checking whether EBITDA is supported by recurring operations. |
| 资产与负债增速比 | If assets grow slower than liabilities, the company may be debt-driven rather than internally accumulating value. |

Diagnosis rule: separate `good liabilities` from `dangerous liabilities`. Payables, deposits, contract liabilities, or customer funds may reflect bargaining power in some industries; overdue payables, interest-bearing debt, hidden debt, or short-term rollover pressure may reflect risk.

### 2.2 Cash Flow Indicators

| Indicator | How to analyze |
| --- | --- |
| 经营性现金流 | Sustained positive operating cash flow usually indicates real business cash generation. Negative cash flow must be interpreted by stage and reason: growth investment, inventory build-up, receivable deterioration, customer credit, or business loss. |
| 投资性现金流 | Negative cash flow may reflect expansion; positive cash flow may reflect asset disposal, investment returns, or shrinking operations. Interpret by strategic context. |
| 筹资性现金流 | Positive cash flow means external financing continues; negative cash flow may mean debt repayment, dividend distribution, or financing contraction. |
| 资产获现率 | Use operating cash flow / assets to test whether assets generate main-business cash. |
| 财务费用覆盖率 | Test whether operating cash flow can cover interest or finance cost pressure. |
| 自由现金流 | Use operating cash flow minus capital expenditure. Sustained negative free cash flow in investment-heavy sectors requires financing and return assessment. |

### 2.3 Growth Structure Indicators

Use growth structure to identify risks hidden behind headline growth.

| Indicator | How to analyze |
| --- | --- |
| 边际效益 | Test whether incremental revenue produces incremental profit. If marginal profit approaches zero or negative, investigate cost structure, pricing, market strategy, and organization efficiency. |
| 运营杠杆 | High fixed-cost ratio means profit is highly sensitive to sales fluctuations. Assess whether capacity utilization or volume growth can dilute fixed costs. |
| 营收与应收账款增速比较 | If receivables grow faster than revenue, investigate credit policy relaxation, early revenue recognition, customer payment pressure, government/customer arrears, and bad-debt risk. |
| 存货增速 | Compare inventory growth with revenue/sales and current assets. Faster inventory growth may imply weak sales, stocking, product mismatch, or liquidity pressure. |

## 3. Analytical Discipline

Follow these rules when writing `客户现状简要剖析`:

- Start with the contradiction, not the metric. Examples: `收入增长但利润未同步改善`, `利润增长但现金流未同步`, `资产扩张但回报下降`, `融资能力尚可但短债集中`, `业务扩张但边际效益走弱`.
- Use at least three years of comparable data when available.
- Use peer comparison where possible, but only after adjusting for business model and accounting caliber.
- Explain whether the issue is performance quality, risk exposure, business structure, operating efficiency, organization mechanism, or data/accounting caliber.
- End each diagnosis point with `管理含义`: what management problem this reveals and what the consulting project should solve.

## 4. Suggested Diagnosis Sequence

1. Establish the company's development stage and strategic context.
2. Test `经营成效`: scale, benefit, returns, turnover, and cash conversion.
3. Test `经营安全`: debt pressure, cash flow sufficiency, and growth structure.
4. Identify contradictions across indicators.
5. Translate contradictions into management pain points.
6. List additional data/interviews needed to confirm root causes.

## 5. Common Management Pain Points

Map evidence to management issues:

| Evidence pattern | Possible management pain point |
| --- | --- |
| Revenue grows but profit and cash do not | Low-quality growth, weak pricing power, low-margin business expansion, poor credit policy, or growth-for-financing behavior |
| Profit relies on non-cash gains | Weak main-business profitability, accounting-quality issue, or insufficient recurring earnings |
| ROE remains low despite asset expansion | Inefficient asset allocation, debt-driven growth, idle assets, or weak investment return management |
| Receivables grow faster than revenue | Loose credit policy, weak customer bargaining power, collection mechanism failure, or public-sector arrears |
| Inventory grows faster than sales | Demand mismatch, product portfolio problem, weak sales operations, or supply-chain planning issue |
| Short debt concentration and weak cash coverage | Refinancing risk, poor treasury planning, or unstable external financing dependence |
| High fixed cost and weak marginal profit | Operating leverage risk, insufficient capacity utilization, or organization/process inefficiency |
