# Methodology Library

Use this reference to choose or adapt frameworks for technical方案 modules. Do not force a famous framework if a bespoke one fits the client better.

## Strategy And Growth

Common frameworks:

- `外部吸引力-内部能力-增长路径`: market attractiveness, customer segments, competitive advantage, resource capability, growth bets.
- `Where to Play / How to Win`: target markets, value proposition, capabilities, economics, risk.
- `三层增长曲线`: core business optimization, adjacent expansion, new business incubation.

Typical outputs: strategic positioning, business portfolio, growth roadmap, investment priorities, strategic KPI system.

## Organization And Governance

Common frameworks:

- `战略-组织-权责-机制`: align structure to strategy, clarify roles, define decision rights, establish operating cadence.
- `总部管控三分法`: strategic control, financial control, operational control.
- `RACI/DRI`: responsible, accountable, consulted, informed; single directly responsible owner.

Typical outputs: organization design principles, org chart, function charters, authorization matrix, RACI, governance meeting system.

## HR And Talent

Common frameworks:

- `岗位-能力-绩效-激励-发展`: role clarity, competency model, performance management, rewards, career development.
- `人才盘点九宫格`: performance and potential segmentation.
- `关键岗位继任`: critical role identification, successor pool, readiness plan.

Typical outputs: position system, competency model, talent inventory, performance plan, incentive scheme, career ladder, training roadmap.

## Performance And Incentives

Common frameworks:

- `战略解码-BSC/OKR-KPI-激励闭环`: strategy map, objectives, metrics, target setting, review, rewards.
- `经营责任制`: business unit responsibility, controllable indicators, budget linkage, incentive and accountability.

Typical outputs: strategy map, KPI library, annual target decomposition, performance contracts, review cadence, incentive calculation rules.

## Finance And Management Accounting

Common frameworks:

- `预算-核算-分析-改善闭环`: planning, accounting, variance analysis, corrective actions.
- `盈利能力树`: revenue, price, volume, cost, expense, asset efficiency, cash conversion.
- `业务财务融合`: finance partner roles, management reports, decision scenarios, data standards.

Typical outputs: budget management process, management report pack, cost/profitability model, variance analysis template, finance BP mechanism.

## Process And Operating Efficiency

Common frameworks:

- `端到端流程管理`: customer trigger, process owner, cross-functional handoff, SLA, metrics, continuous improvement.
- `Lean / Six Sigma`: value stream, waste elimination, defect reduction, standard work.
- `流程分级`: L1 value chain, L2 process domain, L3 activity, L4 SOP.

Typical outputs: process architecture, pain-point map, optimized process, SOP, process owner mechanism, SLA and metrics.

## Digital Transformation And Data

Common frameworks:

- `业务场景-数据资产-系统能力-运营机制`: scenario definition, data model, system enablement, adoption and governance.
- `数字化成熟度`: strategy, process, data, technology, organization, security.
- `数据治理DAMA启发`: standards, quality, ownership, metadata, lifecycle, security.

Typical outputs: digital roadmap, priority scenarios, data governance model, system requirements, dashboard prototype, adoption plan.

## Marketing, Customer, And Channel

Common frameworks:

- `STP-4P-销售漏斗`: segmentation, targeting, positioning, product, price, place, promotion, conversion funnel.
- `客户生命周期`: acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, expansion, win-back.
- `渠道分层运营`: channel role, capability, incentive, coverage, conflict management.

Typical outputs: market segmentation, customer persona, channel strategy, sales process, account management model, marketing KPI.

## Supply Chain And Operations

Common frameworks:

- `S&OP`: demand planning, supply planning, balancing, executive review.
- `供应链韧性`: supplier portfolio, inventory strategy, logistics network, risk monitoring.
- `采购品类管理`: spend analysis, category strategy, supplier management, sourcing execution.

Typical outputs: planning process, inventory policy, supplier evaluation, procurement category strategy, logistics optimization, operations dashboard.

## Risk, Compliance, And Internal Control

Common frameworks:

- `三道防线`: business ownership, risk/compliance oversight, internal audit assurance.
- `风险识别-评估-应对-监控`: risk taxonomy, scoring, controls, reporting.
- `内控五要素`: control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information/communication, monitoring.

Typical outputs: risk map, control matrix, compliance process, audit plan, issue rectification mechanism.

## Creating A Bespoke Framework

Use a bespoke framework when:

- The client problem spans multiple domains.
- Public frameworks would sound generic.
- The proposal needs a distinctive consulting point of view.

Pattern:

| Framework element | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Short Chinese name that signals the problem, such as `四维组织提效模型` |
| Dimensions | 3-5 dimensions with no overlap |
| Diagnosis | One question per dimension |
| Analysis tool | Interview, data analysis, benchmark, workshop, process mapping, financial model, etc. |
| Output | Concrete consulting deliverable |
| Peer mapping | At least one peer practice or public benchmark related to each key dimension |

Example bespoke framework:

`四维经营提效模型`: growth quality, cost efficiency, organization accountability, data-driven review.

- Growth quality: identify profitable growth segments and customer economics.
- Cost efficiency: isolate structural cost, discretionary cost, and process waste.
- Organization accountability: clarify business owner, finance partner, and support function roles.
- Data-driven review: build monthly operating review with leading and lagging indicators.
