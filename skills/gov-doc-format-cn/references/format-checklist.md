# 党政公文格式检查清单

Primary references:

- GB/T 9704-2012《党政机关公文格式》, current national standard baseline. The National Public Service Platform for Standards lists it as effective and records a 2025-05-30 review conclusion of continuing validity.
- 《党政机关公文处理工作条例》, issued by the General Office of the CPC Central Committee and the General Office of the State Council, effective from 2012-07-01.

Use this checklist when formatting or reviewing output:

## Page

- Paper: A4.
- Margins: top 37 mm, bottom 35 mm, left 28 mm, right 26 mm.
- Body approximation: 22 lines per page, 28 Chinese characters per line.
- Footer page number: 4号宋体 Arabic numeral, with dash marks on both sides.

## Fonts

- Title: 2号小标宋体, centered.
- Body: 3号仿宋_GB2312.
- First-level heading: 3号黑体.
- Second-level heading: 3号楷体_GB2312.
- Third-level heading: 3号仿宋_GB2312, bold.
- Use Chinese font fallbacks only when the named fonts are unavailable.

## Paragraphs

- Main title: centered; insert one blank line after it before正文 begins.
- Body paragraphs: fixed 28 pt line spacing; first-line indent 2 Chinese characters.
- Remove unnecessary spaces inside Chinese text, including accidental spaces between Chinese characters, between Chinese text and digits, and around Chinese punctuation.
- Preserve necessary spaces, especially the customary single space after 篇、章、节 heading markers such as `第一节 XXX`, and spaces inside English terms or phrases.
- Chapter-style subheadings named with 篇、章、节, such as `第一篇`, `第一章`, or `第一节`, should be centered with one blank line above and one blank line below.
- Other subheadings that are not centered must start with a first-line indent of 2 Chinese characters.
- Keep headings with following paragraph when possible.
- Main sending organization, body, attachment note, issuing organization, and date should remain in their original order.

## Common Element Order

1. 份号, 密级和保密期限, 紧急程度 when present.
2. 发文机关标志 when producing full red-head documents.
3. 发文字号.
4. 签发人 when present.
5. Title.
6. 主送机关.
7. Body.
8. Attachment note.
9. Issuing organization signature.
10. Written date.
11. Attachment body if present.
12. Copy-to and printing-office blocks if present.

## Review Notes

- Do not invent missing official elements.
- For scanned PDFs, run OCR before formatting.
- For tables, seals, red-head mastheads, and copy-to blocks, inspect the output visually because text extraction may flatten structure.
