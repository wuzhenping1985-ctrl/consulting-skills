# Market Sizing Methods

## Top-Down

Use when reliable industry totals exist.

Equation examples:

- Market size = total population * penetration * usage frequency * price
- Equipment demand = installed base * replacement rate + new capacity demand
- B2B demand = downstream output * usage intensity * unit price

## Bottom-Up

Use when customer/site counts and unit economics are stronger than aggregate reports.

Steps:

1. Segment customers or use cases.
2. Estimate number of accounts/sites/users.
3. Estimate adoption or penetration.
4. Estimate annual volume per account.
5. Estimate average selling price.
6. Sum segments and test against known supply capacity or company revenues.

## Supply-Side Cross-Check

Use for manufacturing, energy, materials, logistics, and infrastructure:

- Capacity * utilization * yield * price
- Import + domestic production - export + inventory change
- Listed company revenue sum / estimated coverage

## Sensitivity

Always vary the two or three assumptions that drive the result most. Show base, low, and high cases with named assumptions.
