# Industry Research Framework

## Market Boundary

- Product/service definition
- Upstream and downstream scope
- Geography and regulatory jurisdiction
- Customer segments
- Current market versus addressable opportunity
- Time horizon: current, 3-year, 5-year, 10-year

## Demand Side

- End-customer needs and purchasing criteria
- Macro drivers: GDP, demographics, urbanization, income, industrial output
- Sector drivers: penetration, replacement cycle, utilization, policy subsidy, technology adoption
- Demand elasticity and cyclicality
- Customer concentration and switching cost

## Supply Side

- Capacity, utilization, bottlenecks, and expansion plans
- Cost curve and scale effects
- Key inputs and supplier power
- Technology routes and standards
- Entry barriers: capital, licenses, channels, data, talent, patents, brand

## Competition

- Market share and concentration
- Strategic groups
- Differentiation basis
- Pricing conduct
- Channel control
- Profitability and capital discipline
- Potential entrants and substitutes

## Strategic Implications

- Where value is created and captured
- Which segments have the best risk-adjusted attractiveness
- What capabilities are required to win
- Which uncertainties could change the answer
- What moves should be made now versus monitored
