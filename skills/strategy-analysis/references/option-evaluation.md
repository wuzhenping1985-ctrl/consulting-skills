# Strategic Option Evaluation

## Option Definition

Each option must state:

- Target segment or market
- Value proposition
- Business model
- Required capabilities
- Investment and resource needs
- Economic logic
- Key risks
- What the company will stop or deprioritize

## Evaluation Criteria

Use 4-6 criteria:

- Strategic attractiveness
- Customer value and differentiation
- Financial impact
- Capability fit
- Feasibility and execution risk
- Timing and reversibility

## Recommendation Logic

Good recommendations:

- Make a clear choice.
- Explain why alternatives are weaker.
- Identify sequencing.
- Separate immediate moves from conditional moves.
- State leading indicators that would invalidate the recommendation.
