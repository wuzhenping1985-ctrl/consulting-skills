# Strategy Analysis

## Core Workflow

1. Define the strategic question and decision owner.
2. Clarify the objective: growth, profitability, resilience, valuation, market entry, transformation, or resource allocation.
3. Diagnose external context: market attractiveness, customer needs, competitors, regulation, technology, and macro trends.
4. Diagnose internal reality: performance, capabilities, assets, operating model, culture, constraints, and financial capacity.
5. Generate strategic options that are mutually distinct and decision-worthy.
6. Evaluate options against attractiveness, fit, feasibility, risk, economics, and timing.
7. Recommend a coherent choice set: where to play, how to win, capabilities, management systems, and roadmap.

## Output Standard

Use this structure unless the user asks otherwise:

- Strategic question and answer.
- Situation diagnosis: market, customer, competitor, internal performance.
- Strategic options with evaluation criteria.
- Recommended strategy and rationale.
- Capability and resource implications.
- Financial/operational impact logic.
- Roadmap, milestones, risks, and decisions required.

## Reference Loading

- Read `references/strategy-frameworks.md` to select frameworks and avoid superficial use.
- Read `references/option-evaluation.md` when comparing strategic options or building recommendation logic.

## Guardrails

- Do not use every framework available; select the few that clarify the decision.
- Do not call a list of initiatives a strategy unless it includes choices and tradeoffs.
- Do not recommend growth without specifying profit logic, capabilities, timing, and risks.
- Separate "must win" choices from "monitor" options.
