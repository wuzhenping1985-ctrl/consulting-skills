# Consulting Deck Structures

## Executive Strategy Deck

1. Executive summary
2. Situation and key question
3. Market / customer / competitor facts
4. Internal capability and performance diagnosis
5. Strategic options
6. Recommended direction
7. Financial and operational impact
8. Implementation roadmap
9. Risks, decisions required, and next steps
10. Appendix

## Industry Research Deck

1. Executive view of industry attractiveness
2. Market boundary and sizing
3. Demand drivers
4. Value chain and profit pool
5. Competitive landscape
6. Policy and technology shifts
7. Scenario outlook
8. Strategic implications
9. Appendix and source notes

## Financial Diagnosis Deck

1. Executive diagnosis
2. Performance snapshot
3. Revenue bridge
4. Margin bridge
5. Cost structure and operating leverage
6. Working capital and cash conversion
7. ROIC / value creation
8. Benchmark gaps
9. Management action plan
10. Risks and data gaps

## Proposal Deck

1. Client context and objectives
2. Our understanding of the problem
3. Proposed approach
4. Workstreams and methods
5. Deliverables
6. Timeline and governance
7. Team
8. Assumptions and commercial terms
