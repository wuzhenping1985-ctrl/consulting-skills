# Problem Structuring

## Problem Statement

Write the problem as:

> How can [client] achieve [objective] by [timeframe] given [constraints]?

Clarify:

- Decision owner
- Objective and metric
- Timeframe
- Scope boundary
- Constraints
- Required deliverable

## Issue Tree Patterns

Profit issue:

- Revenue
- Cost
- Assets/capital
- Risk and sustainability

Growth issue:

- Market growth
- Share gain
- Price/mix
- New products
- New geographies
- Channels and partnerships

Operating model issue:

- Governance
- Organization and roles
- Process
- Systems and data
- Talent and incentives
- KPIs and management cadence

## Hypothesis Plan

For each hypothesis define:

- Why it might be true
- Data required
- Analysis method
- Interview questions
- Expected output
- Decision implication
