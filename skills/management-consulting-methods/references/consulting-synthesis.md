# Consulting Synthesis

## Finding Formula

Use:

1. Observation: what the evidence shows.
2. Interpretation: why it matters.
3. Implication: what the client should do or decide.

Example:

> Receivable days increased from 62 to 91 over four quarters, concentrated in two distributor segments; this suggests collection discipline and channel credit policy, not overall customer demand, are the primary cash pressure points.

## Pyramid Principle

- Lead with the answer.
- Group supporting points into mutually distinct reasons.
- Order by decision relevance.
- Push detailed evidence to appendix when it slows the argument.

## Executive Summary Pattern

Use 3-5 bullets:

- Finding
- Evidence
- Implication
- Recommended action
- Decision required or next step
