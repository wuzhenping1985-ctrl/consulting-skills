# Proposal And SOW Structure

## Proposal Flow

1. Cover and context
2. Understanding of client challenges
3. Project objectives
4. Proposed approach
5. Workstreams
6. Deliverables
7. Timeline and governance
8. Team
9. Relevant experience
10. Assumptions, exclusions, and commercial terms

## SOW Clauses To Specify

- Scope of services
- Client responsibilities
- Deliverables and acceptance criteria
- Project timeline
- Governance and meetings
- Data access and confidentiality dependencies
- Change control
- Out-of-scope items
- Fees, expenses, and payment milestones when requested

## Acceptance Criteria

For each deliverable define:

- Format
- Audience
- Review cycle
- Minimum content
- Data/source dependencies
- Sign-off owner
